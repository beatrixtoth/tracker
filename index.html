Understanding the run types▸
Easy×1 pts/km
Aerobic base + recovery
Relaxed, conversational running that builds your aerobic base and supports recovery.
Long run×1.3 pts/km
Endurance + race-day toughness
Your longest run of the week, building endurance and race-day resilience.
Tempo×1.6 pts/km
Raises your threshold
A sustained, comfortably hard effort that raises your threshold.
Speed×1.8 pts/km
Top-end speed + efficiency
Short, fast repeats with easy recovery to improve speed and efficiency.
Shakeout×1 pts/km
Keeps legs loose, adds nothing
A short, easy jog to keep your legs loose without adding fatigue.
Race day×3 pts/km
The one it's all for
Run with your practiced pace, fuelling, and hydration. Enjoy race day.
